# Canceling Your Subscription

You can cancel your Flowdesk subscription at any time. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

## How to cancel

Only the account owner can cancel. On the Business plan, Admins can also cancel.

1. Go to **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.
2. Select **Cancel subscription**.
3. Tell us why you're leaving. This is optional, but it helps us improve Flowdesk.
4. Select **Confirm cancellation**.

We'll email a confirmation to the account owner, showing the date your access ends.

## When your access ends

Your access ends when your current billing period ends. This date is shown as your **access end date** on the Subscription page.

- **Monthly plans:** access continues until the end of your current monthly billing period.
- **Annual plans:** access continues until the end of your annual term, even if that's many months away.

Your team can keep working normally until then. All plan features remain available, including integrations and automation rules.

### Your subscription still shows as Active

This is expected. A canceled subscription stays **Active** until the paid period ends, with a cancellation date shown alongside it. Once the period ends, the status changes to **Canceled**.

For example, if you're on a monthly plan and cancel on November 1, with your billing period running from November 1 to December 1:

| Date             | What happens                                                           |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| November 1       | Your cancellation is recorded. Your status stays Active.               |
| November 2 to 30 | You and your team keep full access.                                    |
| December 1       | Access ends and your status changes to Canceled. No invoice is issued. |

## Timing your cancellation

Renewal payments can be taken at any time on your renewal date. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least one day before that date.

If you cancel on or after your renewal date, the renewal charge still applies. You keep access through the new billing period that charge paid for, and cancellation takes effect when it ends.

## Refunds after canceling

Canceling doesn't refund the current period. We don't offer refunds for unused time on monthly plans.

If you're on an annual plan and paid within the last 30 days, you may be eligible for a refund. See [Refunds and Duplicate Charges](refunds-and-duplicate-charges.md).

## Unpaid invoices

Canceling doesn't clear what you already owe. Any open or overdue invoices remain payable, and an unpaid invoice can still lead to suspension. See [Failed Payments and Overdue Invoices](failed-payments.md).

## What happens to your data

### During your remaining paid period

Nothing changes. All your data stays available and editable.

### For 90 days after access ends

Your data is retained, but your projects become read-only. You can still sign in, download invoices, and export your data from **Settings > Account > Export data**. Integrations stop syncing and automation rules stop running as soon as access ends.

We recommend exporting anything you need before the 90 days are up, particularly if you're moving to another tool.

### After 90 days

Your project data is permanently deleted and your account is closed. This can't be undone. We keep billing records, such as invoices and payment history, for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

## Moving your team to another tool

If you're canceling because your team is moving to another tool, it helps to plan the move around your access end date rather than around the day you cancel. Canceling early doesn't shorten your access, so you can cancel as soon as you've made the decision and still use the rest of your paid period for the transition. On a monthly plan, that may only give you a few weeks, so start early if you have a lot of projects. On an annual plan, you may have months of paid time left, and there's no need to rush.

Start by deciding what you actually need to take with you. Most teams find that open tasks and recent projects matter far more than years of completed work, although you may want to keep a full export for your records anyway. Then run a test export from **Settings > Account > Export data** while your projects are still fully editable. CSV is usually the easiest format to import into another tool, and JSON keeps more of the structure, such as the links between tasks and their comments. Large accounts can take a few hours to export, so don't leave this until your last day.

Next, look at anything that runs without someone watching it. Automation rules and integrations keep working until your access ends and then stop without further warning, so anything that depends on them should be moved or replaced before that date. Calendar integrations are a good example, because people tend to notice they've stopped only when an event goes missing. Let your team know the date as well, so nobody is caught out when projects become read-only.

Finally, take one last export close to your access end date, so it includes your most recent work. You can still export during the 90 days after access ends, but your projects will be read-only by then, so any changes need to be made first.

## Changing your mind

### Before your access ends

Go to **Settings > Billing > Subscription** and select **Keep subscription**. The cancellation is removed, your subscription continues as if nothing happened, and you'll be charged as normal on your renewal date.

### After your access ends

Within the 90-day retention period, you can start a new subscription from **Settings > Billing > Subscription**. Your data is restored as soon as your first payment succeeds. You'll pay for the new plan from that day, and your new billing period starts then.

After 90 days your data is gone, so you'd need to sign up again and start fresh.

## Canceling versus closing your account

These are two different things.

- **Canceling** stops future billing. Your account stays open until the 90-day retention period ends.
- **Closing your account** deletes your account and data without waiting for the retention period. See [Account Settings](account-settings.md#closing-your-account).

If you just want to pay less, consider [downgrading instead](subscription-changes.md#downgrading-your-plan).

## Can the chat assistant cancel for me?

No. The chat assistant can confirm your cancellation date and when your access ends, but it can't cancel a subscription. Use the steps above, or [open a support ticket](support-tickets.md) if you can't reach the Billing page.
